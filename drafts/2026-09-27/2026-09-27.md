**原文：**
> You can’t automate what you can’t measure. This means that evals are one of the gates to diffusion of AI in the enterprise. 
> 
> We can test our deterministic processes through software, but most enterprises have no useful way of understanding how their non-deterministic processes are working today. Specifically the work that agents are doing for them.

All changes, upgrades, and deployments are downstream from good evals.
> 
> Not only are we going to get vastly more domain specific evals over time for the labs and across the industry, but every enterprise will also need a clear sense of how agents are performing in their environment as well. Huge opportunity.

**中文解读：**
Aaron Levie 指出，企业 AI 落地的核心瓶颈不是模型能力，而是 evals（评估体系）。传统软件流程是确定性的，可以用测试覆盖；但 agent 的工作是非确定性的，企业目前缺乏有效手段去衡量 agent 在自身环境中的表现。没有好的 evals，企业就无法知道什么在起作用、什么坏了、什么改进了，所有部署和升级都无从谈起。他认为未来不仅会出现更多领域专用的 evals，每个企业也需要建立自己的 agent 评估能力，这是一个巨大的机会。

**大白话：**
你想让 AI 帮你干活，但你得先能判断它干得好不好。就像请了个新员工，没有考核标准，你根本不知道他行不行。企业用 AI 也一样，没有评估体系，就不敢真正放手让它干。
